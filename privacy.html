Pipbloom

Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Pipbloom keeps your habits on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. I can't see what you track, and I don't want to.

What the app stores, and where

Your trackers, the days you check in, and your settings are saved on your iPhone or iPad, in storage the app shares only with its own widgets. Nothing is sent to me or to anyone else.

Pipbloom doesn't include analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code from other companies.

iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync, your trackers are copied to your own private iCloud storage so your devices stay in step. That data is held by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. I have no access to it.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. I don't receive your name, email or payment details, only an anonymous confirmation from the App Store that a purchase is valid.

Exports you share

When you export your data as a spreadsheet, the file goes only where you choose to send it.

This website and the waitlist

If you join the waitlist, the form collects your email address, and anything else you choose to type in. It's used only to send you the beta invitation and a note on launch day, and it's never sold or shared. The form is run by Tally, and this site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security. To be removed from the waitlist, email me and I'll delete your details.

Children

Pipbloom isn't aimed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. A change that collects more data than described here would be announced in the app first.
